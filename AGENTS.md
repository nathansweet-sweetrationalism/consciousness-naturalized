Apply the **current** audit policy from https://github.com/nathansweet-sweetrationalism/sweet-rationalism-research/blob/main/AGENTS.md and https://github.com/nathansweet-sweetrationalism/sweet-rationalism-research/blob/main/docs/ARTICLE_QUALITY_GATES.md. Whenever updating this article:

1. Preserve the complete exact original text and publish only revisions approved by Nathan Sweet. Nathan explicitly authorized publication of the complete lossless WordPress revision history on 2026-11-10. Continue to exclude private source-verification working ledgers, unpublished dialogue, third-party PDFs and credentials unless independently authorized.
2. Treat all claims as scoped, revisable and dependent on operational bridges. Keep `CLAIM_DEPENDENCY_MAP.json` a graph of claim conditions, rivals and possible losses, never a graph of frozen conclusions.
3. Verify original publisher/source passages, quoted speakers, context, registration and scope before making or removing attributions. Distinguish metadata matches from abstract checks and full-text passage confirmation.
4. For empirical work, compare competing mechanisms and preregistered countercases; for formal work, verify types, domains, quantifiers, units, source-to-equation translations, countermodels and machine-checker output.
5. Synchronize the website article, this repository's `article.html`, the article entry in the parent `sweet-rationalism-research` repository, linked revision notes, versions and Zenodo records only in a validated owner-approved release. Verify readback HTML hashes and actual live links, and never invent a DOI or badge.
6. Maintain visible full `https://doi.org/...` URLs for actual article DOI identifiers, with the official badge immediately on its next line with exactly one preceding `<br>` and none after, respecting current Kadence rendering. Distinguish article version and concept DOIs.
7. Preserve one consolidated References section after all appendices and dated Revision Notes; include the original public-scholarly repository/code links when genuinely relevant and never suggest endorsements or reciprocal citation.
8. Ensure the article has the four named canonical framework links, an 80–160 word near-top `Bottom line.` before the abstract, working public audit/diff links, external links opening in new tabs, internal links opening in the current tab, and no research boilerplate in card previews.
9. Never create an inbound public-indexable link to this article while it remains protected/noindex/unlisted. On release, separately check WordPress Unlist option, Yoast noindex and signed-out robots/canonical/sitemap behavior.
10. Rerun all relevant executable tests; report both passed checks and unresolved human or evidential gates. A solver result or a source identity lookup is not verification of phenomenal constitution.
