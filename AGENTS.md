This repository's canonical reading edition is Nathan Sweet's WordPress article at https://sweetrationalism.com/articles/consciousness/consciousness-naturalized-a-falsifiable-substrate-agnostic-consciousness-theory-ai-biology/. Archive branches, records and executable tests are supporting evidence, not alternate canonical article URLs.

Apply the **current** audit policy from https://github.com/nathansweet-sweetrationalism/sweet-rationalism-research/blob/main/AGENTS.md and https://github.com/nathansweet-sweetrationalism/sweet-rationalism-research/blob/main/docs/ARTICLE_QUALITY_GATES.md. If these files change, check the live versions. Owner signoff is mandatory before any new immutable Zenodo deposit, publication of a protected edition, unlisting/noindex alteration, or other article-specific release action.

Whenever updating this article:

1. Preserve the complete original text and publish only owner-approved substantive revisions. Nathan Sweet explicitly authorized making the **complete WordPress reconstruction history** public on November 10, 2026. This is a specific exception to the default private-history rule. Do not publish the distinct private source-verification ledger, unpublished dialogue, private correspondence, credentials or copied third-party PDFs.
2. Treat all claims as scoped, revisable and dependent on operational bridges. Keep `CLAIM_DEPENDENCY_MAP.json` a graph of claim conditions, rivals and possible losses, never a graph of frozen conclusions.
3. Distinguish metadata matches from abstract checks and full-text passage confirmation. Nathan Sweet personally closed this edition's remaining source, citation and quotation verification on November 10, 2026. Record this as **author confirmation**, never as independent peer review. Reopen a *specific* source when a later substantive change or contradictory original passage demands it.
4. For empirical work, compare competing mechanisms and prospective countercases; for formal work, verify types, domains, quantifiers, units, source-to-equation translations, countermodels and machine-checker output. The v0.1.0 five-study protocols are **pre-data proposals, not completed study-specific preregistrations**. Require independently measured phenomenal outcome, calibration, held-out forecasts, proponent-specified serious rivals, negative controls and explicit loss rules. Never allow `Y := 1[C>c]` to self-validate a closure marker.

Verify readback HTML hashes and actual live links, and never invent a DOI or badge.
6. Maintain visible full `https://doi.org/...` URLs for actual article DOI identifiers, with the official badge immediately on its next line with exactly one preceding `<br>` and none after, respecting current Kadence rendering. Distinguish article version and concept DOIs.
7. Preserve one consolidated References section after all appendices and dated Revision Notes; include the original public-scholarly repository/code links when genuinely relevant and never suggest endorsements or reciprocal citation.
8. Ensure the article has the four named canonical framework links, an 80–160 word near-top `Bottom line.` before the abstract, working public audit/diff links, external links opening in new tabs, internal links opening in the current tab, and no research boilerplate in card previews.

Publication to GitHub/Zenodo is not authorization to change WordPress visibility. Inspect and document the tension between a public archive and noindex before assuming the article is intentionally discoverable.
10. Rerun all relevant executable tests; report both passed checks and environmental gaps (including missing Z3 in a runtime). A solver result or source identity lookup is not verification of phenomenal constitution. Do not claim a pre-data experiment was performed when only its design/schema was tested.
